AI-enabled workflow

From signed agreement to validated upload.

Key-account agreements meant matching agreed prices to the right SKUs by hand. I designed a Microsoft Copilot agent that updates or compares the Adobe Commerce pricing workbook, preserves unrelated data, flags exceptions and leaves an audit trail for human review before IT upload.

Outcome
Adopted for day-to-day use, with documentation and a practical handover

Founder

Seven years of taking briefs from idea to installation.

I founded and ran a floral event business, delivering luxury commissions, weddings, installations and more than 300 workshops. I managed each project from first brief to on-site delivery, balancing creative intent, budgets, materials, suppliers and deadlines.

Clients
Dior, Chanel, Bvlgari, Coach, Lane Crawford, Sulwhasoo, Farfetch, Hublot

Brief and budget

Make the trade-offs part of the design.

Each project started with the theme, brand guidelines and budget. I developed options through mood boards, flower selections and visual proposals, and costed materials, transport, labour and time. Where budgets were tighter, I changed flower choices, used more space and line, or simplified the build.

Method
Mood board → flower selection → proposal → costing

From visual proposal to finished installation.

For retail launches, I set out arrangement positions, scale, colour palette and flower selections in a visual proposal, then delivered the installation. At SENREVE the florals had to sit within a wider shop design and budget; at Lane Crawford, around the product displays.

SENREVE’s finished installation matched the proposed direction
