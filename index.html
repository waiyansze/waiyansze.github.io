Built within Microsoft 365 Copilot Agent Builder, without Studio, connectors, APIs or write-back to live systems. The agents prepare reviewable outputs; people approve and apply changes.

Market and currency
Check that the account’s market, currency and price group agree. Flag inconsistencies.
Evidence before correction
Recommend a replacement only when exactly one mapped answer is supported. Otherwise: “Manual review required”.
Variable inputs
Confirm freight and exchange rates before each pricing run.
Data preservation
Preserve source files and unrelated workbook data. Record changes separately.
Source reconciliation
Account for every row as processed, excluded, out of scope or requiring review. Nothing is silently dropped.

Before processing

My operational checkpoint

  • Product compatibility
  • Billing
  • Processing requirements
  • Urgent orders
  • System information

≈10% of monthly orders need a product-related correction before processing. Illustrative proportion, not an order count. Proforma and other arrangements are extra work on top.

What an early check can prevent

  1. CustomerAn unsuitable item, extra charges or a delay in getting what they need
  2. Internal teamsReturns, redelivery and debit or credit administration
  3. ChannelA return to manual email ordering

Order quality

Catch the issue before it travels.

Before orders move into processing, I review product compatibility, billing details and system information. I resolve exceptions and coordinate proforma or urgent arrangements so each order is ready for the next team.

Why it matters
Incorrect orders create extra work for customers and internal teams, and can push customers back to email ordering
