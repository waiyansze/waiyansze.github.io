01 · B2B eCommerce operations

Making a complex order channel work better

Managing the day-to-day UK eStore means seeing beyond a single screen. The real work happens between systems, data and people.

01

Observe

Start where the friction appears.

Customer questions, unusual charges and failed order states are signals. I first establish what happened, who is affected and whether the issue is isolated or systemic.

02

Trace

Follow the hand-offs, not just the screen.

I connect storefront behaviour with SAP order and invoice data, Dynamics 365 customer records and the operational process around them to locate where reality diverged from expectation.

03

Resolve

Turn diagnosis into coordinated action.

A third-party SAP application created duplicate freight across 65 orders, including 15 already invoiced. I investigated the pattern and coordinated the correction with business and technical teams.

04

Improve

Use the learning to make the channel stronger.

  • +10.81pponline order adoption
  • Top 2global market utilisation
  • 134technical and service requests coordinated
