Rules the agents follow, for example

Written by me

  1. CurrencyA GBP price group belongs to a UK or Northern Ireland account; EUR to the Republic of Ireland. Anything else is flagged.
  2. EvidenceA new group is recommended only when exactly one mapped replacement is proven. Otherwise: Manual Review Required.
  3. VariablesFreight and exchange rate are confirmed by a person before every pricing run, because both change.
  4. ReconcileEvery source row ends up processed, excluded, out of scope or in review. Nothing is dropped silently.

Illustrative wording. Mappings, customers and values stay internal.

AI controls · in detail

A check that runs again beats a one-off clean-up.

The wrong groups and stores came from manual entry, handover gaps and a registration flow that makes mistakes easy. Cleaning them once would not stop them coming back, so I turned the rules into agents that anyone trained can run.

My role
Problem, rules, controls, testing, user guides, training
Boundary
The agents never write to live systems; people make the corrections
