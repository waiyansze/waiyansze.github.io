Overview

Current role · UK B2B eCommerce · Digital operations

Keeping a connected order journey moving.

I oversee the day-to-day operation of the UK eStore across Adobe Commerce, SAP ERP, Dynamics 365 and ServiceNow. My work combines order quality, cross-system investigation and practical workflow improvement.

Order quality · Cross-system diagnosis · Workflow improvement

01 / Quality

Catch the issue before it travels.

Every order is checked against customer, product, pricing and system information before processing. Around 10% require correction at this stage, helping prevent avoidable problems later in fulfilment, invoicing or returns.

Exceptions resolved before downstream processing

02 / AI-enabled workflow

From signed agreement to validated upload.

Key-account agreements required agreed prices to be matched manually to the correct SKUs. I designed a Microsoft Copilot agent to update or compare the Adobe Commerce pricing workbook, preserve unrelated data, flag exceptions and produce a clear audit trail for human review before IT upload.

  1. Map agreed prices to SKUs
  2. Confirm freight & FX
  3. Update or compare workbook
  4. Review exceptions
  5. Approve for IT upload

Outputs

Updated workbook · Validation and exception report · Summary and change log

Outcome

The workflow was adopted for day-to-day use, supported by clear documentation and a practical handover.

03 / Diagnosis

Find the pattern, then control the impact.

A series of order-level freight exceptions revealed a wider cross-system issue. I established the scope, separated orders already invoiced, connected the supporting evidence and coordinated corrections while normal ordering continued.

Operational outcome

65 affected orders identified · 15 already invoiced · normal ordering maintained

Escalation

I escalated the evidence to SAP and global ebusiness teams for technical investigation and assessment of the potential risk to other markets.

Ownership

I owned the impact assessment, evidence validation and response coordination. Specialist teams handled the technical fix.

04 / Visibility & data quality

Make operational information easier to trust.

Recurring problems often began with information that was missing, outdated or difficult to see. I traced those gaps to their source and improved the information used by operational teams and business users.

Selected improvements

  • Restored Matomo reporting and corrected an inaccurate shipment status.
  • Refreshed the UK product cross-reference, removing retired products and adding newly available items.
  • Cleaned customer and price-group records and strengthened onboarding guidance for system and billing accounts.

Outcome

Clearer operational data, fewer avoidable enquiries and less corrective administration.

Working approach

Establish the evidence, identify the responsible hand-off and close the information gap at its source.
