Making the UK eStore more reliable.

01 / Quality control

Catch issues early.

I check customer, product, pricing and system information before orders progress. Around 10% need correction, reducing the risk of fulfilment, invoicing and return problems.

  • ~10%corrected before processing

02 / Workflow

Turn business rules into a controlled AI workflow.

I designed a Microsoft Copilot agent that updates Adobe Commerce pricing workbooks from key-account PDFs and compares workbook versions. It confirms variable inputs, preserves unrelated data, flags exceptions and creates an audit trail before IT upload.

  1. Confirm freight & FX
  2. Update or compare
  3. Validate exceptions
  4. IT upload

Adoption evidence

Adopted by my manager; I documented the process and trained her to use it.

03 / Cross-system diagnosis

Trace the cause across systems.
