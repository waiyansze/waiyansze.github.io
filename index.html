Overview

Current role · UK B2B eCommerce · Digital operations

Keeping a connected order journey moving.

I oversee the day-to-day operation of the UK eStore across Adobe Commerce, SAP ERP, Dynamics 365 and ServiceNow. My work combines order quality, cross-system investigation and practical workflow improvement.

Order quality · Cross-system diagnosis · Workflow improvement

01 / Quality

Catch the issue before it travels.

Every order is checked against customer, product, pricing and system information before processing. Around 10% require correction at this stage, helping prevent avoidable problems later in fulfilment, invoicing or returns.

Exceptions resolved before downstream processing

02 / AI-enabled workflow

From signed agreement to validated upload.

Key-account agreements required agreed prices to be matched manually to the correct SKUs. I designed a Microsoft Copilot agent to update or compare the Adobe Commerce pricing workbook, preserve unrelated data, flag exceptions and produce a clear audit trail for human review before IT upload.

  1. Map agreed prices to SKUs
  2. Confirm freight & FX
  3. Update or compare workbook
  4. Review exceptions
  5. Approve for IT upload

Outputs

Updated workbook · Validation and exception report · Summary and change log

Outcome

The workflow was adopted for day-to-day use, supported by clear documentation and a practical handover.

03 / Diagnosis

Find the pattern, then control the impact.

A series of order-level freight exceptions revealed a wider cross-system issue. I established the scope, separated orders already invoiced, connected the supporting evidence and coordinated corrections while normal ordering continued.

Operational outcome

65 affected orders identified · 15 already invoiced · normal ordering maintained

Escalation

I escalated the evidence to SAP and global ebusiness teams for technical investigation and assessment of the potential risk to other markets.

Ownership

I owned the impact assessment, evidence validation and response coordination. Specialist teams handled the technical fix.

04 / Visibility & data quality

Make operational information easier to trust.

Recurring problems often began with information that was missing, outdated or difficult to see. I traced those gaps to their source and improved the information used by operational teams and business users.

Selected improvements

  • Restored Matomo reporting and corrected an inaccurate shipment status.
  • Refreshed the UK product cross-reference, removing retired products and adding newly available items.
  • Cleaned customer and price-group records and strengthened onboarding guidance for system and billing accounts.

Outcome

Clearer operational data, fewer avoidable enquiries and less corrective administration.

Working approach

Establish the evidence, identify the responsible hand-off and close the information gap at its source.

01 / Space · Ensō House · London

From an idea to an operating art space in 3.5 months.

I joined Ensō House as its first employee, before there was a venue, operating model, website or booking journey. Working within a lean, founder-led setup, I developed practical options, secured approval and carried the chosen direction through to launch.

Business launch · Project delivery · Operations

02 / From concept to plan

Make the idea workable.

I sourced the property and translated an evolving vision into a workable launch plan, bringing together the space, budget, suppliers, digital journey and operating requirements.

I prepared the renovation proposal for the landlord, producing the floor plans and spatial drawings to communicate the intended layout.

03 / Building the space

Build for everyday use.

I planned and managed the renovation, controlled the budget, obtained quotations and coordinated contractors. I also produced the floor plans and spatial mock-ups internally, avoiding the need for a separate interior design service.

04 / Connecting the experience

Connect the space and the booking journey.

I translated the founder’s creative direction into a website and user-experience brief, managed the external production partner and set up the third-party booking journey. After launch, I continued to manage the website and booking operations.

05 / Developing the operating model

Give the space more ways to work.

I proposed and implemented membership and private-hire models alongside the programme offer. When membership alone provided limited commercial support, I helped shift resources towards venue hire and ikebana-led experiences to create a more resilient mix.

06 / Exhibitions and programmes

One space, different programmes.

Over the following 15 months, I contributed to more than 50 exhibitions, workshops and programmes through a combination of curation, production, operational support, hosting and teaching. Programme collaborators included Sarabande, Central Saint Martins and Sogetsu.

07 / Representation and outcomes

Carry the work beyond the venue.

I represented Ensō House through an ikebana demonstration at Strawberry Hill House Flower Festival.

3,000 sq. ft. venue · launched in 3.5 months · delivered within the agreed budget · 50+ programmes

Working approach: turn an evolving idea into clear options, connect the dependencies and carry the approved direction through.

01 / Flowers · The Floral Flowerist

Seven years of taking briefs from idea to installation.

I founded and ran a floral event business, delivering luxury commissions, weddings, installations and 300+ workshops. My work ranged from floral design to complete event concepts and production.

I managed each project from the first brief to on-site delivery, balancing creative intent, budgets, materials, suppliers and deadlines.

Selected collaborations: Dior, Chanel, Bvlgari, Coach, Lane Crawford, Sulwhasoo, Farfetch and Hublot.

02 / Understand the brief

Turn a brief into a visual direction.

I started with the theme, brand guidelines and budget, then developed options through mood boards, flower selections and visual proposals.

03 / Design within the budget

Make the trade-offs part of the design.

I costed materials, transport, labour and time. Where budgets were tighter, I adjusted flower choices, used more space and line, or simplified the build.
